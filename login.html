The VEIT Tool uses embedded AI/ML to replicate federal evaluation standards using the 0-5 Master Rating Guide. It auto-ranks vendors across weighted performance areas, generates natural-language summaries explaining each score, and highlights areas of risk or concern. Each vendor is reviewed for added value, identified weaknesses, and positioned relative to the most advantageous offeror.

COs can evaluate hundreds of PPQs in minutes with confidence, traceability, and audit-ready justification.
